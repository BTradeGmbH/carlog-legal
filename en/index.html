Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Without an account

The data is stored in a database inside the app. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud backup if you have turned it on. Apple's privacy policy applies to that backup.

With sync (optional)

When you sign in, we store your email address and a copy of your data with a hosting provider whose data centre is in Switzerland. An email service provider in the EU sends the sign-in code on our behalf. Each account can only see its own data. All transfers are encrypted.

Subscription

The first 10 entries are free. From the 11th entry and for sync you need CarLog Pro (yearly subscription, CHF 5 per year in Switzerland). Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment data. A subscription management service provider (based in the USA) processes a random identifier and the purchase status so the app knows whether Pro is active. Data processing agreements are in place with all service providers; for the USA, the standard contractual clauses apply.

No tracking

CarLog contains no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking across apps or websites. This website does not load anything from third parties either.

Deletion and your rights

  • You delete the data on your iPhone by deleting the app.
  • You delete your account and all data on the server in the app under Settings → Sync → Delete account.
  • You have the right to access, correct and delete your data under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection and, where applicable, the GDPR. To do so, write to the address below.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I back up my data? With CarLog Pro: Settings → Sync → Sign in and sync. Your data is then stored safely on a server in Switzerland. Without a subscription it stays in your iPhone's iCloud backup.
  • New iPhone? Install CarLog and sign in under Settings → Sync with the same email address. Your data comes back automatically.
  • Cancel the subscription? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CarLog.
  • Why is «Consumed» different from «Spent»? «Spent» is what you paid. «Consumed» estimates what the kilometres you drove actually cost.
